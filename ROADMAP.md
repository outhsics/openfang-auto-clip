# Roadmap

Goal: a local clip factory people actually post from. Not another docs site.

## v0.6 — Ship the clip (now)

## v0.8 — Daily driver

Caption templates, folder batch, a quality score tied to hook-in-3s and face occupancy.
No mobile app. No hosted SaaS.

Silence is worse than a small release. Ship clips, then talk.
