# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this repository to be a useful engineering space for contributors, evaluators, and operators. Expected behavior includes:

- discussing technical tradeoffs directly and respectfully
- assuming good faith while still challenging weak claims
- keeping bug reports and reviews concrete
- helping maintain docs, tests, and release quality

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or personal attacks
- discriminatory language
- doxxing or privacy violations
- intentionally misleading claims about project capabilities

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or restrict participation when behavior undermines the project or community.

## Maintainer Rule

If a conflict appears to be about product claims, benchmark validity, or legal positioning, prefer evidence and exact wording over escalation.
